### What went well

- Each member of the group was able to work on their part of the project (each screen that was given to them during the planning phase).
- There was effective conversation during classes (11/4 and 11/6) about the project where each member was able to help each other if questions or opinions were asked.

### What did not go well

- We were unable to integrate the three separate screens each member worked on, thus there is no game in Production.
- We had lapses in communication and did not effectively communicate on the appearance of some screens or how to connect the individual components created (connects with the point made above).
- Our finished products at the end of the sprint do not have much functionality and is mainly just UI.

### What puzzled us or what we want to try

- What we want to try is integrating all the screens together so that the player moves through the starting screen, the mage class selection screen, and the mage customization screen seamlessly.
- We want to figure out a way to create "global variables" so that the font is consistent throughout the game and the character sprite is consistent with what the player selects in the customization screen as the game progresses.

## 5. Root Cause


**Problem:** Poor communication or lack of acknowledgement towards progress.

**Why?** We all have different schedules and are often unable to work on the project until other work from other classes are completed. This also contributes to why many of us cannot meet on certain days because of our different busy schedules.

**Why?** Leaving the project to the final days before the review, which leads others having to wait on the person to finish before we can discuss integration and connecting the components together.

**Why?** Failing to communicate if one cannot work on the project (for one reason or another) or needing help and not asking the other members. Lack of "checking in" and sharing progress being made towards finishing the Issue.

**Root cause:** Communication between all members of the group.
